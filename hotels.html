At the wedding

Ski Tip Lodge

Nine rooms in the lodge itself, in the 1880s stagecoach building where the wedding happens. Breakfast included, and you can walk upstairs at the end of the night. It goes first, so book first.

Rooms

Nine, all different

Small, old, wood-panelled and full of character. Not a hotel: an inn with a fireplace and a shared sitting room.

Distance

Zero

You are already there. Worth it for anyone who does not want to think about driving.

Note

Book early

Nine rooms across a wedding of forty-odd people. First come, first served.

Keystone Resort

The village

Everything below is resort-managed, on the shuttle route, and covered by the discount. River Run is the liveliest, Lakeside the quietest, Mountain House the closest to Ski Tip.

Hotel

Keystone Lodge & Spa

The full-service option: proper hotel, spa, restaurant, lake views. Easiest choice if you want a hotel rather than a condo.

Hotel

Inn at Keystone

Simpler and cheaper, in Mountain House, five minutes from the lodge. Good value for a two-night stay.

Condos

River Run Village

Dakota Lodge, Jackpine, Silver Mill, Arapahoe, Buffalo Lodge and Expedition Station. Kitchens, pools and hot tubs, right by the gondola and the restaurants.

Condos

Lakeside Village

Quieter, on the water, a short shuttle from River Run. Better for families who want space.

Condos

The Springs and Tenderfoot

Larger units at the base of the mountain, good for groups sharing.

Nearby

If Keystone fills up

The discount will not apply here, but these are all within twenty-five minutes and often cheaper.

Breckenridge

25 minutes

Where the after party is. Hotels and rentals on and around Main Street. Best choice if you want a proper town to walk around.

Dillon and Frisco

10–15 minutes

Chain hotels by the lake and the interstate, plus the brewery where Wednesday starts.

Silverthorne

15 minutes

The practical option: outlet shops, supermarkets and the cheapest beds in the county.
