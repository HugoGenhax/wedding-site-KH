Three places

Where

Wednesday night starts easy, with drinks at the Dillon Dam Brewery ten minutes down the road. Come straight from the airport if that's how the day works out.

The wedding itself is at Ski Tip Lodge in Keystone on Thursday, from five until ten. It started life as an 1880s stagecoach stop, became Colorado's first ski lodge in 1947, and still has the original stone fireplace and the Snake River running behind the garden. Ceremony, dinner and dancing all happen there, and we come back on Friday for brunch.

At ten the lodge says goodnight, and anyone still standing comes with us over Swan Mountain Road to , about twenty-five minutes away, to finish the night properly.

Getting here & staying

Stay

Fly

Denver International

About 100 miles west on I-70, roughly 1 hour 45 minutes in good weather. Epic Mountain Express runs shuttles door to door if you'd rather skip the rental.

Sleep

Keystone or Breckenridge

Ski Tip Lodge itself has nine rooms — book early. Otherwise stay in Keystone village, or in Breckenridge if you'd rather be near the after party. We've held rooms under "Katie & Hugo" through May 1.

Altitude

9,200 feet

Keystone is high, and Breckenridge is higher. Drink far more water than feels necessary, go easy on the first night's drinks, and give yourself a day to adjust if you can.

If altitude has troubled you before, see your doctor at least three weeks ahead about preventive medication. It is usually started two days before you arrive, so it needs planning.

More

Two longer lists

Every hotel with our discount, and things to do around Keystone — hikes, rafting, gold mines and the rest.

Weather

July in the Rockies

Low 70s and bright by day, mid 40s once the sun drops behind the ridge. Wildflowers everywhere. Afternoon thunderstorms blow through fast, so pack a layer and something for rain.

What to wear

Dress code

Black tie optional, mountain edition. Long dresses, dark suits, tuxedos if you own one. Bring a wrap or an overcoat for the terrace — the temperature drops the moment the sun goes behind the ridge.

The ceremony is on grass, so block heels or flats will serve you far better than stilettos. If you're taking your cues from the palette, we're in olive, cream, and gold.
